# Classic Rice Krispie Treats

The standard low-heat method keeps the marshmallow mixture soft rather than tough.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp butter
- 10 oz marshmallows, or about 4 cups mini marshmallows
- 6 cups crisp rice cereal
- Pinch salt, optional
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, optional

## Instructions

1. Line or lightly grease an 8-by-8-inch or 9-by-13-inch pan.
2. Melt the butter over low heat.
3. Add the marshmallows and stir over low heat until just melted and smooth.
4. Remove from the heat.
5. Stir in the optional salt and vanilla.
6. Fold in the cereal until evenly coated.
7. Press gently into the prepared pan without compacting excessively.
8. Cool about 30 minutes before cutting.
