# Professional Chicago Italian Beef

The professional workflow is roast, chill, slice extremely thin, then reheat the slices gently in hot jus.

## Beef

- 4 to 6 lb top round

### Dry Seasoning by Meat Weight

- Kosher salt: 1.5 percent of meat weight
- Coarse black pepper: 0.5 percent of meat weight
- Dried oregano, to taste
- Dried thyme, to taste
- Crushed red pepper, to taste
- Ground coriander, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste

## Jus

- 3 qt beef stock
- Up to 2 cups reserved beef drippings, as available
- Garlic, to taste
- Dried oregano, to taste
- Crushed red pepper, to taste
- Fennel seed, to taste
- Bay leaf
- Worcestershire sauce, to taste
- 1/2 tsp MSG

## For Serving

- Turano or Gonnella-style Italian rolls
- Giardiniera
- Sweet peppers, optional

## Instructions

1. Season the top round evenly with the measured salt and pepper plus the remaining dry seasonings.
2. Roast at 275°F until the center reaches 128 to 130°F.
3. Rest 30 minutes.
4. Chill at least 4 hours, preferably until fully cold and firm.
5. Slice across the grain about 1/16 inch thick.
6. Combine the stock, drippings, garlic, oregano, red pepper, fennel, bay, Worcestershire, and MSG.
7. Simmer the jus 20 to 30 minutes and adjust seasoning.
8. Hold the jus between 160 and 170°F.
9. Reheat sliced beef in the hot jus only 1 to 2 minutes, just until warmed through.
10. Pile onto rolls with giardiniera and optional sweet peppers.
11. Serve dry, wet, or fully dipped according to preference.

## Notes

Do not boil the sliced beef. The low-temperature jus preserves the tender rare-roast texture while heating the sandwich for service.
