# Professional Stir-Fry Master Formula

A repeatable framework rather than a single fixed dish. The key is staging: protein, vegetables, aromatics, and sauce are handled separately so everything reaches the pan at the right moment.

## Base Formula

### Protein

Use 12 oz of one:

- Chicken thigh or breast, thinly sliced
- Beef, sliced thinly across the grain
- Pork, thinly sliced
- Shrimp, peeled and deveined
- Firm tofu, pressed and cubed

### Vegetables

Use about 4 cups total, choosing 3 to 5:

- Bell pepper
- Broccoli
- Snow peas
- Sugar snap peas
- Mushrooms
- Bok choy
- Green beans
- Carrot
- Onion
- Zucchini
- Cabbage

Cut vegetables so they finish cooking at roughly the same time.

### Aromatics

- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely chopped or grated
- 2 scallions, sliced, optional

---

## Master Stir-Fry Sauce

### Ingredients

- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1/3 cup water or unsalted stock
- 1/4 tsp white pepper, optional

### Steps

1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together until the cornstarch is fully dispersed.
2. Rewhisk immediately before adding to the pan.

The sauce should be savory and glossy, not thick like gravy.

---

## Optional Protein Marinade

Useful for chicken, beef, or pork.

### Ingredients

- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp neutral oil

### Steps

1. Toss with the sliced protein.
2. Rest 15 to 20 minutes while preparing the vegetables.

For beef, an optional 1/8 tsp baking soda can be added for extra tenderness. Do not use more.

## Cooking Method

### 1. Prepare Everything First

Stir-frying moves too quickly to prep while cooking.

Have ready:

- Protein
- Vegetables grouped by cooking time
- Garlic and ginger
- Sauce
- Neutral high-smoke-point oil

### 2. Sear the Protein

1. Heat a wok or large skillet until very hot.
2. Add 1 tbsp neutral oil.
3. Add the protein in a single layer.
4. Leave it undisturbed long enough to brown.
5. Toss or turn and cook until about 80 to 90 percent done.
6. Transfer to a plate.

Cook in two batches if necessary. Crowding is worse than taking an extra two minutes.

### 3. Cook the Vegetables

1. Add another small amount of oil if needed.
2. Add the hardest vegetables first, such as carrots, broccoli, or green beans.
3. Add medium-cooking vegetables next.
4. Add delicate greens last.
5. Stir-fry until brightly colored and crisp-tender.

If the vegetables need help cooking through, add 1 to 2 tbsp water and cover for 30 to 60 seconds, then uncover and let the water evaporate.

### 4. Add Aromatics

1. Push the vegetables aside or clear the center.
2. Add garlic and ginger.
3. Cook only 15 to 30 seconds, until fragrant.

Do not brown the garlic.

### 5. Recombine

Return the protein and accumulated juices to the pan.

### 6. Sauce

1. Rewhisk the sauce.
2. Pour it around the hot sides of the wok or skillet.
3. Toss continuously for 30 to 60 seconds.
4. Stop as soon as the sauce turns glossy and lightly coats everything.

### 7. Finish

Taste and adjust with one of:

- Soy sauce for salt
- Rice vinegar for brightness
- Chile crisp or fresh chiles for heat
- Sesame oil for aroma

Serve immediately.

---

## Scaling Rule

For every 12 oz protein plus 4 cups vegetables, use one batch of master sauce.

For a larger wok batch:

- 24 oz protein
- 8 cups vegetables
- Double the sauce

Do not double the food in a small skillet. Cook in batches instead.

## Variation Paths

### Beef and Broccoli

- Beef
- Broccoli
- Onion
- Add 1 tsp dark soy sauce to the master sauce

### Chicken and Snow Peas

- Chicken
- Snow peas
- Mushrooms
- Bell pepper
- Finish with scallions

### Shrimp and Bok Choy

- Shrimp
- Bok choy
- Bell pepper
- Mushrooms
- Add a little rice vinegar at the end

### Tofu and Green Beans

- Crisp the tofu first
- Green beans
- Mushrooms
- Onion
- Add chile crisp at service

## Make-Ahead

- Sauce: up to 5 days refrigerated
- Vegetables: cut 1 day ahead
- Protein: slice 1 day ahead
- Marinade: 15 to 30 minutes before cooking is sufficient
