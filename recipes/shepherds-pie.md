# Professional Shepherd's Pie

A reconstructed version built around the remembered 1 1/2 pounds of lamb and 2 1/2 pounds of potatoes. The filling is savory rather than sweet, with a deeply browned lamb base and enough acidity and Worcestershire to keep the dish lively.

## Lamb Filling

### Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb ground lamb
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, divided
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, only if needed
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 celery stalks, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup beef or lamb stock
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary, crushed
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar or sherry vinegar
- 1/2 tsp MSG, optional

### Steps

1. Heat a wide skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
2. Season the lamb with about 1 tsp salt and the black pepper.
3. Brown the lamb in a broad layer, allowing a dark crust to develop before breaking it apart.
4. Continue cooking until well browned, then remove excess fat if necessary.
5. Add the onion, carrot, and celery.
6. Cook 6 to 8 minutes until softened and beginning to brown.
7. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
8. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 to 3 minutes, until brick red.
9. Sprinkle in the flour and cook 1 minute.
10. Add the Worcestershire and stock while scraping up any browned bits.
11. Add thyme, rosemary, optional MSG, and remaining salt as needed.
12. Simmer 8 to 10 minutes, until the filling is thick enough to hold a furrow when a spoon is dragged through it.
13. Stir in the frozen peas and vinegar.
14. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and acidity.
15. Transfer to a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.

## Yukon Gold Potato Topping

### Ingredients

- 2 1/2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup whole milk or light cream, warmed
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more for the cooking water
- 1/4 tsp white pepper
- 1 oz finely grated Parmesan, optional

### Steps

1. Cover the potatoes with cold salted water.
2. Bring to a gentle boil and cook until completely tender.
3. Drain thoroughly.
4. Return the potatoes to the hot pot for 1 to 2 minutes to steam off excess moisture.
5. Mash or rice until smooth.
6. Fold in the butter.
7. Add the warm milk or cream gradually.
8. Stir in the egg yolk, salt, white pepper, and optional Parmesan.
9. Keep warm for assembly.

---

## Assembly and Bake

1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Spread the potato topping over the lamb filling.
3. Roughen the surface with a fork to create ridges that will brown.
4. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and browned on top.
5. Broil 1 to 3 minutes if additional color is needed.
6. Rest 10 to 15 minutes before serving.

## Why This Version Works

- Lamb is browned hard before the vegetables go in, preserving a meaty flavor rather than steaming it.
- Tomato paste and flour are cooked before the stock, eliminating raw flavors.
- Peas go in late so they remain green.
- Vinegar is added at the end so the filling does not taste flat.
- Egg yolk strengthens the potato crust enough to brown well without making it heavy.

## Make-Ahead

Assemble up to 24 hours ahead. Refrigerate covered. Bake from cold at 375°F for about 40 to 50 minutes, uncovering for the final 20 minutes.
