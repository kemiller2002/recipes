# Castleton-Style Fried Spinach

A reconstruction of the light, crisp fried spinach style served at Bravo in Castleton.

## Spinach

- 9 to 10 oz fresh baby spinach, washed and dried extremely well

## Batter

- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 2 tbsp finely grated Romano
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 3/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- Pinch white pepper, optional
- 3/4 cup ice-cold club soda

## Finish

- 2 tbsp finely grated Parmesan
- 1/4 tsp garlic salt
- Pinch dried oregano

## Lemon-Garlic Mayonnaise

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 1/2 garlic cloves, very finely minced or grated
- 1 1/2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- Pinch kosher salt
- Small splash olive oil

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, salt, and olive oil. Refrigerate 30 minutes.
2. Heat frying oil to 350°F.
3. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, Romano, baking powder, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and optional white pepper together.
4. Whisk in the ice-cold club soda just until a thin batter forms.
5. Work in very small batches of about 6 to 8 spinach leaves.
6. Coat lightly in batter and fry about 20 to 30 seconds, just until crisp.
7. Drain immediately.
8. Toss or dust while hot with the Parmesan, garlic salt, and oregano mixture.
9. Serve immediately with the chilled lemon-garlic mayonnaise.

## Notes

The spinach must be very dry before battering. Water on the leaves both weakens the batter and makes frying dangerous.
