# Sun God BBQ Sauce

A competition-style sauce designed to read as classic barbecue first, with the Mayan-inspired elements kept subtle enough that the sauce still feels familiar.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup finely diced onion
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo
- 1 tsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp finely grated orange zest
- 1 tbsp fresh orange juice
- 1 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup water

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook about 5 minutes, until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook about 1 minute.
5. Add the tomato sauce, ketchup, vinegar, honey, brown sugar, chipotle, adobo sauce, salt, pepper, smoked paprika, cumin, cinnamon, orange zest, orange juice, cocoa, and water.
6. Bring to a gentle simmer.
7. Simmer 25 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until cohesive and slightly thickened.
8. Blend until smooth.
9. For a polished competition texture, strain through a fine-mesh strainer.
10. Cool and refrigerate overnight before judging or serving.

## Notes

- The intended profile is recognizable barbecue first, with chipotle, orange, spice, and cocoa appearing as background complexity.
- Avoid increasing the cocoa or cinnamon enough that either becomes individually identifiable.
