# Cayenne Cucumber Kimchi

A one-pound cucumber kimchi built for pickling cucumbers when cabbage and gochugaru are not available.

## Ingredients

### Cucumbers

- 1 lb pickling cucumbers
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 small carrot, julienned, optional

### Seasoning Paste

- 1 to 1 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp grated apple or pear, optional
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil, optional
- Sesame seeds, optional

## Instructions

1. Quarter the cucumbers lengthwise and cut into roughly 2-inch pieces.
2. Toss with the kosher salt and rest 20 to 30 minutes.
3. Rinse quickly and drain very well.
4. Mix the cayenne, paprika, fish sauce, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, ginger, optional fruit, rice vinegar, and optional sesame oil into a paste.
5. Toss the cucumbers with the paste, scallions, and optional carrot.
6. Pack into a clean container.
7. Eat immediately for a crisp fresh version, or refrigerate 2 to 4 hours before serving.
8. For light fermentation, leave at room temperature 6 to 12 hours, then refrigerate.

## Notes

This is intentionally a cayenne-based adaptation, not a claim to replicate the flavor of gochugaru exactly.
