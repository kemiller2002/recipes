# Fresh Pepper Hot Sauce

A flexible cooked hot sauce that works with whatever fresh hot peppers are available.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz fresh hot peppers, stems removed
- 3 to 4 garlic cloves
- 1/2 medium onion
- 3/4 cup white vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 to 2 tsp sugar or honey, optional
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice, optional

## Instructions

1. Roughly chop the peppers, garlic, and onion. Fine chopping is unnecessary.
2. Combine the peppers, garlic, onion, vinegar, water, salt, and optional sugar or honey in a saucepan.
3. Bring just to a boil.
4. Reduce to a gentle simmer and cook 15 to 20 minutes, until the vegetables are very soft.
5. Blend until smooth. For a thinner sauce, strain after blending.
6. Stir in the lime juice, if using.
7. Adjust salt, vinegar, water, or sweetness to taste.

## Louisiana-Style Simplification

For a sharper, simpler version, use mostly peppers, vinegar, salt, and a little garlic, with little or no onion or sugar.
