# Greek Shrimp Pasta with Feta

A quick Mediterranean pasta where the feta is added off heat so it stays creamy and distinct instead of turning grainy.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz pasta
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup dry white wine, optional
- 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
- 4 oz feta, crumbled
- 1/4 cup Kalamata olives, sliced
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- Zest and juice of 1/2 lemon

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water until al dente.
2. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
3. Season the shrimp with salt, pepper, and oregano.
4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet.
5. Cook the shrimp about 60 to 90 seconds per side, just until nearly cooked through. Remove.
6. Add the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil.
7. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook about 30 seconds.
8. Add the cherry tomatoes and optional white wine.
9. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, until the tomatoes begin to soften.
10. Add the reserved pasta water and simmer briefly.
11. Toss in the pasta and shrimp.
12. Turn off the heat.
13. Add the feta, olives, lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley.
14. Toss gently and serve immediately.

## Notes

Adding the feta after the heat is off preserves its texture and keeps the sauce from becoming grainy.
