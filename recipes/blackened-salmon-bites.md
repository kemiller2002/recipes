# Blackened Salmon Bites

Small pieces of salmon cooked quickly in a hot skillet so the spices darken and become aromatic without burning.

## Ingredients

- Salmon, cut into bite-size pieces
- Olive oil, for coating
- Paprika, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Onion powder, to taste
- Dried thyme, to taste
- Dried oregano, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Cayenne, optional
- Kosher salt, to taste
- High-smoke-point oil such as avocado, canola, or grapeseed, for the pan
- Lemon juice, for finishing
- Butter, optional for basting

## Skinless Method

1. Toss the salmon lightly with olive oil and the seasonings.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a thin film of high-smoke-point oil.
3. Place the most heavily seasoned side down.
4. Sear about 2 minutes.
5. Flip and cook another 1 to 2 minutes, until just cooked through.
6. Finish with lemon juice.

## Skin-On Method

1. Season the flesh side.
2. Heat the skillet over medium-high heat with a thin layer of high-smoke-point oil.
3. Place the salmon skin-side down.
4. Cook 3 to 5 minutes, until the salmon is roughly 70 to 80 percent cooked and the skin is crisp.
5. Flip and cook the seasoned flesh side only 30 to 90 seconds.
6. Optionally baste briefly with butter.
7. Finish with lemon juice.

## Serving Suggestion

Serve over a baked sweet potato with garlic-butter green beans or asparagus.
