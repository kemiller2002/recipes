# Party Creole Jambalaya

A flexible party version built around the trinity, tomato paste, smoked sausage, chicken, and shrimp.

## Ingredients

### Rice Base

- 2 cups long-grain white rice
- 2 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes or tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato paste

### Trinity and Aromatics

- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 red or yellow bell pepper, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced

### Seasoning

- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 to 1 tsp cayenne
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

### Proteins

- 1 1/2 to 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 12 to 14 oz smoked sausage, sliced
- 1 lb peeled and deveined shrimp

### Finish

- Butter or olive oil
- Sliced green onions
- Hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Brown or roast the chicken and sausage separately so each develops good color. Reserve rendered fat for the rice base.
2. In a heavy pot, cook the onion, celery, and bell pepper in the rendered fat until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook briefly.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook until it darkens to a deep brick red.
5. Add the smoked paprika, cayenne, thyme, and bay leaf.
6. Add the rice, chicken stock, and crushed tomatoes.
7. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the rice is tender.
8. Fold the chicken and sausage back in.
9. Cook the shrimp separately just until about 90 percent done, roughly 2 to 3 minutes, then fold in at the end so it does not overcook.
10. Finish with butter or olive oil, green onions, and hot sauce.

## Brown-Rice Adaptation

1. Cook 2 cups brown rice separately with 3 cups chicken stock in a rice cooker.
2. Build the trinity, tomato paste, and spice base in the main pot.
3. Add 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes plus 1/2 to 3/4 cup stock and simmer about 5 minutes.
4. Fold in the cooked brown rice.
5. Simmer 5 to 10 minutes.
6. Cover, remove from the heat, and rest 10 minutes.
7. Add the cooked proteins and finish as above.

## Party Service Note

For a choose-your-own-protein setup, hold the chicken, sausage, and shrimp separately and let guests add what they want. Shrimp can be held briefly, about 15 to 20 minutes, near 140°F in a little butter sauce.
