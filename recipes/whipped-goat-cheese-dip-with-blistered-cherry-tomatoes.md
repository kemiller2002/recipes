# Whipped Goat Cheese Dip with Blistered Cherry Tomatoes

## Main Recipe

### Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 8 oz cherry tomatoes
- 6 oz soft goat cheese
- 4 oz whole-milk, low-moisture mozzarella, freshly shredded
- 3 oz cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup packed fresh basil leaves
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ⅛ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice

### Steps

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over high heat.
2. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they blister and begin to release their juices.
3. Remove from heat and cover tomatoes in pan covered for 10 minutes.
4. Combine the goat cheese, mozzarella, cream cheese, basil, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, kosher salt, and black pepper in a food processor.
5. Process until completely smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed to ensure all ingredients are fully incorporated.
6. Spoon the whipped cheese mixture into a shallow serving dish.
7. Top with the blistered tomatoes.
8. Drizzle the reserved olive oil and tomato juices from the skillet over the top.
9. Garnish with fresh basil leaves.

---

## Assembly

1. Serve with club crackers or water crackers
