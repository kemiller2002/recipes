# Mediterranean Crossroads Short Ribs

This is a recovered recipe concept from an earlier conversation. The flavor architecture and method survived, but the exact quantities did not. It is intentionally preserved as a concept rather than padded with invented measurements.

## Braise Framework

Use a classic Italian short-rib braise built from:

- Beef short ribs
- Onion
- Carrot
- Celery
- Garlic
- Tomato paste
- Crushed San Marzano tomatoes
- Dry red wine
- Beef stock

## Moroccan Layer

Keep the spices restrained so the dish still reads as braised beef first:

- Cumin
- Coriander
- Ground ginger
- Tiny amount of cinnamon

## Herbs and Citrus

- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Parsley
- Mint
- Preserved lemon, or fresh lemon when preserved lemon is unavailable

## Method

1. Brown the short ribs deeply.
2. Build an Italian soffritto with onion, carrot, celery, and garlic.
3. Caramelize the tomato paste.
4. Add the cumin, coriander, ginger, and a very small amount of cinnamon.
5. Deglaze with dry red wine.
6. Add crushed tomato and beef stock.
7. Return the short ribs and braise until completely tender.
8. Strain or refine the braising liquid as desired.
9. Reduce the sauce until glossy.
10. Finish with cold butter or good olive oil.
11. Brighten with preserved lemon or fresh lemon.

## Garnish

- Chopped parsley
- Toasted almonds
- Lemon zest
- Mint, used lightly

## Suggested Sides

- Creamy Parmesan polenta
- Olive-oil mashed potatoes

## Menu Pairing

The original concept specifically paired the short ribs with a classic, unchanged Caprese salad rather than altering the Caprese to match the braise.
