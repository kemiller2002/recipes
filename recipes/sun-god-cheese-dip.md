# Sun God Cheese Dip

A reconstructed competition dip designed around one rule: it must taste like an excellent cheese-and-roasted-corn dip before the judge notices anything unusual.

Ancho, lime, honey, smoked Gouda, and cocoa are background architecture, not headline flavors.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 4 oz sharp white cheddar, finely shredded
- 1 oz smoked Gouda, finely shredded
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 1 cup roasted corn, divided
- 2 scallions, very thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ancho chile powder
- 1/4 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp dark honey
- 1 tsp finely grated lime zest
- 2 tsp fresh lime juice
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper

## Steps

1. Reserve 1/3 cup of the roasted corn for texture.
2. Blend or mash the remaining 2/3 cup roasted corn with the sour cream, honey, lime juice, ancho, and cocoa until mostly smooth.
3. In a bowl, beat the cream cheese until smooth.
4. Fold in the white cheddar and smoked Gouda.
5. Fold in the corn mixture.
6. Add the reserved whole roasted corn, scallions, lime zest, salt, and black pepper.
7. Mix just until evenly distributed.
8. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
9. Taste cold and adjust salt or lime before serving.

## Competition Balance

The target hierarchy is:

1. Cheese
2. Roasted corn
3. Ancho and lime
4. Smoke
5. Honey
6. Cocoa

If the cocoa is clearly identifiable, there is too much. Start with 1/4 tsp. Increase only to 3/8 tsp after the dip has rested overnight if more bass note is needed.

## Optional Garnish

- A few roasted corn kernels
- Thin scallion curls
- Very light dusting of ancho
- Lime zest

Avoid visible cocoa powder. The cocoa should be discovered through depth, not announced visually.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with neutral, crisp carriers:

- Corn tortilla chips
- Thin pita crisps
- Plain crostini

## Reconstruction Note

The original concept preserved cream cheese, white cheddar, a small amount of smoked Gouda, roasted corn, ancho, 1/4 to 1/2 tsp cocoa, honey, and lime. The sour cream, exact ratios, and complete method were rebuilt to make it a balanced chilled competition dip.
