Lechón Asado

A slow-roasted Cuban-style pork shoulder built around a garlic-citrus mojo.

Pork

  • 1 bone-in pork shoulder, 5 to 7 lb
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil

Mojo

  • 12 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 1/2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 cup sour orange juice
  • or 1/2 cup orange juice plus 1/2 cup lime juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. Cut deep slits 1 to 2 inches apart across the pork.
  2. Season the pork with the kosher salt.
  3. Blend the garlic, oregano, cumin, black pepper, citrus juice, and olive oil into the mojo.
  4. Work or inject the mojo into the slits and coat the exterior thoroughly.
  5. Cover with the sliced onion.
  6. Refrigerate 12 to 24 hours.
  7. Heat the oven to 300°F.
  8. Place the pork and marinade in a covered roasting pan or Dutch oven.
  9. Roast 4 to 5 hours, until very tender and around 195°F internally.
  10. Uncover and raise the oven to 425°F.
  11. Roast another 15 to 20 minutes to crisp the exterior.
  12. Rest before slicing or pulling.
  13. For extra crisp edges, shred portions of the pork and broil 3 to 5 minutes before serving.

Serving Suggestions

Serve with Cuban black beans, white rice, plantains, or pickled onions.
