Potaje de Garbanzos con Chorizo

A hearty Spanish chickpea stew with chorizo, potato, vegetables, greens, smoked paprika, and a final splash of sherry vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 oz Spanish chorizo, sliced or diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 (14 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 5 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 cups spinach or kale
  • 1 to 2 tsp sherry vinegar
  • Olive oil, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Add the chorizo and cook 5 to 7 minutes to render its fat.
  3. Remove about one-third of the chorizo and reserve for serving.
  4. Add the onion and cook about 5 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  6. Add the smoked paprika and cook about 15 seconds.
  7. Add the crushed tomatoes and cook 5 to 8 minutes.
  8. Add the carrots, bell pepper, potato, chickpeas, chicken stock, and bay leaf.
  9. Simmer uncovered 25 to 30 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the broth has developed.
  10. Season with salt and black pepper.
  11. Stir in the greens and cook 2 to 3 minutes.
  12. Add the sherry vinegar.
  13. Discard the bay leaf.
  14. Serve topped with the reserved chorizo and a drizzle of olive oil.

Optional Upgrades

  • Add a ham hock, jamón bone, or Parmesan rind during the simmer.
  • Mash about 1/2 cup of the chickpeas into the broth for a thicker texture.
  • Serve with crusty bread.
