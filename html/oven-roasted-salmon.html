Oven-Roasted Salmon

A minimal salmon method designed around high heat and internal temperature rather than a rigid clock.

Ingredients

  • Salmon fillet or portions
  • Olive oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Lemon
  • Fresh herbs, butter, or garlic, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Pat the salmon dry.
  3. Rub lightly with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Place skin-side down on a baking sheet or roasting pan.
  5. Roast about 8 to 10 minutes per inch of thickness. A typical portion often takes 10 to 12 minutes.
  6. Pull the salmon about 5°F before the desired final temperature.
  7. Rest 2 minutes.
  8. Finish with lemon and optional butter, garlic, or herbs.

Temperature Guide

  • Medium: 120 to 125°F
  • Fully cooked but still moist: 130 to 135°F
