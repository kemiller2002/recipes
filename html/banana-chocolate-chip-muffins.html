Banana Chocolate Chip Muffins

Soft banana muffins loaded with semisweet chocolate chips. This entry preserves the ingredient formula and key baking parameters from the original Preppy Kitchen recipe while restating the method for this collection.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (180 g)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar (113 g)
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips, plus extra for topping if desired
  • 3 medium ripe bananas, about 1 cup mashed
  • 1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled (75 g)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (5 ml)

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, and sugar together in a medium bowl.
  3. In a large bowl, mash the bananas until mostly smooth. Mix in the egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
  4. Fold the dry mixture into the banana mixture just until only a few streaks of flour remain.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips, stopping as soon as the batter is evenly combined. Avoid overmixing.
  6. Divide the batter among the 12 muffin cups. Add a few extra chocolate chips to the tops if desired.
  7. Bake for 17–20 minutes, until the centers are set and a toothpick inserted into a muffin comes out without wet batter.
  8. Let the muffins cool briefly in the pan, then transfer them to a rack.

Notes

  • Very ripe bananas give the best flavor, sweetness, and texture.
  • Milk, dark, or white chocolate can be substituted for semisweet chocolate.
  • For a variation, add about 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans.
  • A 1/2 tsp cinnamon addition works well for a warmer flavor.
  • The original recipe notes that the muffins keep for roughly 5–7 days in an airtight container.
  • They can be frozen for up to 3 months when wrapped well.
