Chicken, Andouille, and Shrimp Gumbo

A dark-roux gumbo with no tomatoes. Chicken provides body, andouille provides smoke, shrimp goes in only at the end, and the okra is browned separately before joining the pot.

Ingredients

Proteins

  • 2 1/2 to 3 lb bone-in chicken thighs
  • 12 to 16 oz andouille sausage, sliced
  • 1 lb peeled and deveined shrimp

Okra

  • 12 to 16 oz fresh okra, preferably 1 lb, sliced about 1/4 inch thick
  • Rendered fat or neutral oil, as needed

Dark Roux

  • 3/4 cup neutral oil
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour

Trinity and Aromatics

  • 2 cups diced onion
  • 1 1/2 cups diced green bell pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups diced celery
  • 5 to 6 garlic cloves, minced

Liquid and Seasoning

  • 7 to 8 cups chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 to 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp cayenne
  • 1 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 to 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice, optional
  • Kosher salt, to taste

Finish

  • Sliced scallions
  • Chopped parsley
  • Cooked white rice
  • Filé powder for the table, optional

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. In a heavy Dutch oven, brown the chicken well in batches. Remove.
  3. Brown the andouille in the same pot. Remove, keeping some aside for the later finish.
  4. Add the okra and sauté 10 to 15 minutes until browned and noticeably less slimy. Remove.
  5. Add the 3/4 cup oil if needed, then whisk in the flour.
  6. Cook the roux over controlled heat, stirring constantly, until it reaches a deep dark-chocolate color, about 25 to 45 minutes.
  7. Immediately add the onion, bell pepper, and celery. Stir into the roux and cook until softened.
  8. Add the garlic and cook briefly.
  9. Gradually whisk in the stock.
  10. Add the bay leaves, thyme, oregano, paprika, cayenne, black pepper, white pepper, Worcestershire, hot sauce, optional allspice, the chicken, and most of the sausage.
  11. Simmer gently for 75 to 90 minutes.
  12. Remove the chicken, pull the meat from the bones, and return the meat to the pot.
  13. Return the browned okra during the final 30 to 40 minutes of cooking.
  14. Add the reserved sausage and simmer another 20 to 30 minutes as needed for the gumbo to come together.
  15. Add the shrimp during the final 3 to 5 minutes, just until cooked through.
  16. Adjust salt, hot sauce, and consistency.
  17. Finish with scallions and parsley and serve over rice.

Notes

  • Do not add tomatoes.
  • With a full pound of okra in the pot, filé is optional and is best offered at the table rather than boiled in the gumbo.
