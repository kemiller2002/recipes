Crispy Shredded Onion Rings

A thin, lacy onion fritter style designed to emphasize crisp edges rather than a thick batter shell.

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet onions
  • Kosher salt, for drawing out moisture
  • 1/4 cup finely grated Parmesan, plus more for finishing
  • 2 to 3 tbsp all-purpose flour or potato starch
  • 1 large egg or egg white
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Garlic powder, optional
  • Paprika, optional
  • Neutral frying oil
  • Flaky salt, optional for finishing

Instructions

  1. Julienne or coarsely grate the onions into thin strands.
  2. Toss lightly with kosher salt and rest 10 to 15 minutes.
  3. Squeeze the onions very aggressively to remove as much liquid as possible.
  4. Mix with the egg, flour or potato starch, 1/4 cup Parmesan, black pepper, and optional garlic powder or paprika.
  5. Heat a shallow layer of neutral oil over medium-high heat.
  6. Drop small piles of the onion mixture into the oil and press them very thin.
  7. Leave them undisturbed until the underside is deep golden and lacy.
  8. Flip and crisp the second side.
  9. Drain briefly.
  10. Finish immediately with additional Parmesan and optional flaky salt.

Notes

Keeping the piles thin and removing excess onion moisture are the two main controls for crispness.
