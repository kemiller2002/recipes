Classic Pot Roast

A classic oven-braised chuck roast with vegetables and a reduced pan jus.

Ingredients

  • 1 beef chuck roast, 3 to 4 lb
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced or diced
  • 4 to 6 carrots, cut into large pieces
  • 1 to 1 1/2 lb potatoes, cut into large pieces
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup red wine, or additional broth
  • Bay leaf and thyme, to taste
  • Worcestershire sauce or soy sauce, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 300°F.
  2. Pat the roast dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven and sear the roast deeply on all sides. Remove.
  4. Add the onion and cook until softened and beginning to brown.
  5. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook briefly.
  6. Deglaze with the wine, scraping up the fond.
  7. Add the broth, herbs, and optional Worcestershire or soy.
  8. Return the roast to the pot.
  9. Cover and braise 3 to 3 1/2 hours, until fork-tender.
  10. Add the carrots and potatoes during the final 60 to 90 minutes so they do not overcook.
  11. Remove the roast and vegetables.
  12. Reduce the braising liquid 10 to 15 minutes if a more concentrated jus is desired.

Slow Cooker Alternative

Cook on low 8 to 9 hours or high 4 to 5 hours, adding the vegetables at a point that preserves their texture.
