Rich Drinking Chocolate

A richer, thicker drink than ordinary hot cocoa. Finely chopped dark chocolate provides body and intensity, while cocoa powder deepens the chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 4 oz dark chocolate, 60–70%, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1–2 Tbsp granulated sugar, to taste
  • 1/8 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Optional for a Thicker, Spanish-Style Texture

  • 1 tsp cornstarch

Optional for Extra Richness

  • Replace 1/2 cup of the whole milk with 1/2 cup heavy cream

Directions

  1. In a saucepan, whisk together the cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and cornstarch, if using.
  2. Add about 1/4 cup of the cold milk and whisk until completely smooth with no cocoa lumps.
  3. Whisk in the remaining milk.
  4. Heat over medium-low heat until steaming, stirring frequently. Do not let it come to a hard boil.
  5. Add the finely chopped dark chocolate.
  6. Whisk until the chocolate is completely melted and the mixture is smooth and glossy.
  7. Simmer very gently for 2–4 minutes, whisking frequently. If using cornstarch, the chocolate will noticeably thicken.
  8. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla.
  9. Pour into two mugs and serve immediately.

Preferred Rich Version

For a particularly luxurious version, use:

  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 4 oz 70% dark chocolate
  • 2 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/8 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

This produces a silky, intensely chocolatey drink with enough body to feel closer to melted chocolate than conventional hot cocoa.

Adjustments

  • Thinner: Add a splash of hot milk before serving.
  • Thicker: Simmer another minute or increase cornstarch slightly.
  • Less sweet: Use 70–85% dark chocolate and reduce the added sugar.
  • Sweeter: Add sugar 1 teaspoon at a time after the chocolate has melted.
  • Spiced: Add cinnamon, chile, espresso powder, or a small pinch of cayenne while heating the milk.

Reheating

Reheat gently over low heat while whisking. Add a small splash of milk if it has thickened too much in the refrigerator.
