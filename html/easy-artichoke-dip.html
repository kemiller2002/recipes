A simple baked artichoke, mayonnaise, and Parmesan dip with optional mozzarella.

Easy Artichoke Dip

A simple hot dip that comes together from pantry ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 (14 oz) can artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • or 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix the artichokes, mayonnaise, Parmesan, garlic, black pepper, and optional mozzarella.
  3. Spread in a small baking dish.
  4. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until hot and lightly golden.
  5. Serve warm.

Serving Suggestions

  • Sliced baguette
  • Crackers
  • Tortilla chips
  • Raw vegetables
